## Key design decisions

- **Retrieval + spacing + mastery** are the backbone: every encounter after the first is a recall with immediate feedback; words return after 1 day, ~3 days, ~1 week, 2–3 weeks.
- **Positive, informative feedback** ("You heard the *sh* in *ship*!"), never a bare "Wrong"; no shop, leaderboard, lives, streak shaming or pressure timers.
- **Hebrew-specific**: minimal pairs (ship/sheep, three/tree), short vowels, b/d anchors, left-to-right tracking.
- **Parent in the loop**: the research shows apps alone are weak; with an adult they work.
- **Speech is optional**: child speech recognition is wrong 15–25% of the time, so recordings are compared by the child, never used to block progress.
- **Pilot unit:** "All About Me / My Family" (matches the grade-4 goal of giving personal information).

## Evidence caveats
Most strong evidence comes from native-language or adult learners; evidence for young EFL learners is thinner. Items marked *(secondary)* were read through summaries. Each doc lists its sources and gaps.
