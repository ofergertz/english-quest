# Survey: existing kids' English / literacy products — what to copy, what to avoid

> Condensed from a research pass in October 2026. **No game alone has strong independent evidence of improving reading.** The best independent results come from short adaptive practice *plus an adult* (GraphoGame: no effect alone, ES 0.48 with adult involvement) and from speech-based reading tutors (Amira: ES +0.64 in one RCT). Most products target ages 3–8 and native English speakers; nothing found is built for a 9–10-year-old Hebrew speaker learning English. That gap is the opportunity.

## Product notes

| Product | What the child physically does | Evidence | Takeaway |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Duolingo ABC** | Traces letters (guardrails fade, return after 2 misses); repeats narrated mini-story sentences; comprehension Qs | Sponsor-funded 28% gain claim, no details | Fading scaffolds; repeat-after-me |
| **Teach Your Monster to Read** | Builds a monster; mini-games: hear a sound → jump on the letter, shoot asteroids that spell a word, segment CVC words to climb a ladder, "tricky words" as creatures | No independent outcome study found | Best mini-game variety for phonics; tricky-word creatures |
| **GraphoGame (Rime)** | Hear a sound, pick letters/rimes; 10 min/day; 80% mastery rule | EEF RCT (398 pupils): −1 month, "no evidence"; ES 0.48 with adult involvement | Adaptive drill alone isn't enough → add parent/teacher touchpoints |
| **Khan Academy Kids** | Adaptive path guided by characters, books, games; free, no ads | Small UMass RCT (n=49, ages 4–5): literacy 34th→47th percentile | Ad-free, calm; content ends ~grade 2 |
| **Starfall / Reading Eggs / ABCmouse** | Phonics animations / lesson path + egg shop / 8,000 activities + ticket shop | Mostly vendor-linked or correlational | Avoid shop-style currency loops and clutter |
| **Lingokids** | Large menu of songs, videos, mazes, tracing | Self-published, self-reported enjoyment | Star only for *different* activities (no grinding) — good idea; endless menus — bad |
| **Endless Alphabet/Reader** | Drag scattered letters back onto outlines; animation acts out the meaning | None found | Animated meaning is great; mixing sight words and phonics cues confuses (e.g. silent-e) |
| **Kahoot! / Poio** | Bugs that eat letters; mastered words fill a storybook the child reads aloud | Kahoot review: speed scoring → guessing/anxiety | Avoid speed-scored competition for weaker readers |
| **Wordwall** | 34 teacher templates (match, unjumble, balloon pop, maze chase…) | None found | Teachers already use it — export-friendly content helps adoption |
| **Gus on the Go** | Owl guide on a map; flashcards with native audio, then an arcade mini-game | None found | Simple map + guide works; ~90 words only |
| **Amira / ELSA / Novakid** | Child reads aloud, AI listens / pronunciation feedback / live teacher + games | Amira RCT +0.64 (grades 1–4); ELSA mostly teens/adults | Speech is the differentiator — but child ASR error is 15–25% |

## Israeli context
- Ministry programs: Five Minutes of English (grades 1–4), Speak UP, Learn in Tune, Dig into Digital (5–6), Building Blocks literacy guidance, ABLE KIT reading screener (end of grade 4 / start of 5).
- Building Blocks flags sound contrasts hard for Hebrew speakers: feel/fill, bad/bed, b/p, v/w, consonant clusters, silent letters; recommends Hebrew instructions with English sounds.
- *English for Everyone* (Project 720): AI-personalised program rolling out to grades 7–8 from 2026–27; no results published.
- Not verified: digital English games from ECB, CET, Kotar or Galim.

## Patterns recurring in the stronger products
1. Hear a sound → pick the letters (with distractors).
2. Adaptive mastery loop (~80%), unknown items recycled.
3. Drag-to-build words.
4. Blend/segment as a game *action*.
5. Short daily sessions (5–15 min).
6. Narrated decodable mini-stories as the reward.
7. Repeat-after-me / read-aloud with feedback.
8. Gentle error handling: hint → retry → scaffold fades.
9. A mascot the child "teaches" or travels with.
10. Map/path showing progress.
11. Tricky words as characters.
12. Animation that shows meaning.
13. Content unlocks (stories, worlds) instead of a shop.
14. Parent/teacher dashboard.
15. Reward variety, not grinding.

## Anti-patterns
Shops/currency · endless menus · guilt notifications and streak shaming · lives/hearts that block practice · speed-scored ranking · passive video · unproven claims (plan a matched comparison from day one).
